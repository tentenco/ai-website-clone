# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Tenten Website Clone Engine a
respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and kind.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy, client confidentiality, licensing, and source permissions.
- Give credit for work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Welcome questions and make room for contributors with different experience.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith
  argument.
- Publishing private information or client material without permission.
- Impersonation, deceptive attribution, or plagiarism.
- Abuse of project infrastructure for phishing, surveillance, or unauthorized
  cloning.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
community events, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate public moderation need, flag the content through GitHub and avoid
escalating the thread. For a private report, use the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/tentenco/ai-website-clone/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct report`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant. Reports will be
reviewed as privately and promptly as the platform permits. Retaliation against a
reporter or witness is prohibited.

Maintainers who are directly involved in a report must recuse themselves from the
decision whenever another qualified maintainer is available.
